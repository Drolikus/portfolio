An early-stage concept I am still exploring. The interesting part is the moment of truth — a ticket flow that is honest and clear, and win/lose results that never feel ambiguous.

Problem

Clarity at the result

A lottery UI must be unambiguous: what you played, what the draw was, and whether you won — with no room for misreading.

Idea

A calm, honest flow

Pick, confirm, wait, reveal. Each step should be readable and trustworthy, with results that state the outcome plainly.
